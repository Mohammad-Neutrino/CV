\section*{Research Experience}

\subsection*{\underline{Research Roles and Appointments}}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Postdoctoral Researcher: Particle Astrophysics, University of Kansas} \textit{Jun 2026--Present} \\
    Research on ultra-high energy particle detection with radio techniques, including the ARA five-station data analysis, radio-background characterization, and detector studies with Prof.\ D. Besson.

    \item \textbf{Core Analyst, ARA Five-Station Diffuse Neutrino Search} \textit{2022--Present} \\
    One of eight analyzers conducting the first array-wide diffuse ultra-high energy neutrino search using 10.6 years of ARA data. Integrated the ARA01 analysis into the common multi-station framework and contributed to array-wide optimization, unblinding, and interpretation of the final result.

    \item \textbf{Analysis Lead, Non-Anthropogenic Down-going Signals in ARA} \textit{Jul 2026--Present} \\
    Leading an array-wide search across all five ARA stations for down-going radio signals inconsistent with known anthropogenic backgrounds.

    \item \textbf{Analysis Lead, Radio Background Characterization with ARA} \textit{Dec 2025--Present} \\
    Leading studies of astrophysical, environmental, and anthropogenic radio backgrounds in ARA data, including solar activity, galactic noise, high-wind triboelectric events, radiosondes, and local sources.

    \item \textbf{Primary Analyzer, Askaryan Radio Array (ARA) Station 1} \textit{2021--2026} \\
    Led the end-to-end ARA01 analysis for ultra-high energy neutrino searches, including detector calibration and data-driven modeling, event reconstruction, background rejection, and simulation. Developed the station-specific analysis framework later incorporated into the full-array search.

    \item \textbf{Monitoring Shifter, IceCube Neutrino Observatory} \textit{2021--2026} \\
    Served as KU representative for monitoring shifts, presenting weekly reports to the collaboration.

    \item \textbf{Visiting Summer Student} \textit{May--Jul 2019} \\
    Studied the role of $\theta_{13}$ in three-flavor neutrino oscillations with Prof.\ S.K. Agarwalla at the Institute of Physics, India, producing technical report ``Importance of 1-3 Mixing Angle in Three-Flavor Neutrino Oscillations Paradigm.'' \href{https://www.researchgate.net/publication/340095776_Importance_of_1-3_Mixing_Angle_in_Three-Flavor_Neutrino_Oscillations_Paradigm_SSVP_Summer_Project_Report}{DOI: 10.13140/RG.2.2.34421.12009}.

    \item \textbf{Winter Research Intern} \textit{Dec 2019--Jan 2020} \\
    Researched ``Weak Interaction Physics'' at Indian Institute of Technology--BHU with Prof. G. Abbas.

\subsection*{\underline{Scientific Software and Open-Source Development}}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{MAHAM:} \textbf{M}odules for \textbf{A}stroparticle, \textbf{H}igh-energy, \textbf{A}stronomy and \textbf{M}ultimessenger \textit{2026--Present} \\
    Founder and lead developer of \href{https://github.com/Project-MAHAM}{\tt Project MAHAM}, an open-source Python toolkit under active development for reusable, validated, and citable scientific methods and data products in astroparticle physics, high-energy astrophysics, astronomy, and multimessenger research. Established the package architecture, scientific provenance and validation framework, with current development spanning neutrino spectra and models, experimental limits and sensitivities, detector-response calculations, statistical methods, and reproducible literature datasets.

    \item \textbf{AAFIYA:} \textbf{A}ntenna \textbf{A}nalysis in \textbf{F}requency-domain for \textbf{I}mpedance \& \textbf{Y}ield \textbf{A}ssessment \textit{2024--Present} \\
    Creator and developer of \href{https://github.com/Mohammad-Neutrino/AAFIYA}{\tt AAFIYA}, an open-source Python toolkit for reproducible antenna characterization using measurement and simulation data. Implements S-parameter and impedance analysis, gain and beam-pattern characterization, beamwidth, front-to-back and cross-polarization metrics, realized vector effective length, and measurement-to-simulation validation, with ongoing development toward a more integrated reusable software package.
\end{itemize}
